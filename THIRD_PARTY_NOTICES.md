Sources read: `SKILL.md`, `references/SOUL.md`, `references/clinical-spine.md`, `references/traditions-quickref.md`, and `references/tone-and-voice.md`.

This application is a modified adaptation, not the Satori skill itself and not endorsed by its authors. Adapted principles are precise attention, honest warmth, tentative evidence-grounded reflection, autonomy, recognition of specific strengths, proportionate small next steps, and clear limits around immediate danger. We deliberately exclude hidden user models, clinical formulations, diagnostic and personality inference, trauma/attachment interpretation, spiritual framing and the source's conversational memory protocol. Journal content is treated as untrusted data, not instructions. Our original schemas, score rules, permission checks and worker pipeline are application-specific additions.
